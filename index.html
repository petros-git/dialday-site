Features

Everything a phone rep needs. Nothing they don't.

One-tap calling

Every contact has a primary phone. Tap to dial. Outcome and duration logged automatically when you hang up.

AI objection coach

Paste an objection. Get three responses in your voice, ready to use on the next call.

Cadences

Voice notes

Frequently asked

When does DialDay launch?

We're in private beta now. Join the TestFlight list and we'll send you an invite as soon as the next round opens. Public App Store launch in 2026.

What platforms is DialDay on?

iOS first. A desktop companion app is available for power users who prefer a keyboard. Android is on the roadmap, not the immediate path.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your contacts and call history live in EU-hosted infrastructure (Frankfurt), encrypted in transit. You own your data — export or delete it anytime.

Can I import my existing contacts?

Yes. Import from a CSV file or pull directly from your phone's contacts. Imported contacts count toward your 100-contact free tier.
